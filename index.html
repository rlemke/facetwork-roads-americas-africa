World low-zoom roads: Americas and Africa

Each map shows the road network in bands that appear as you zoom in: motorways first, then trunk, primary and smaller roads. Built from OpenStreetMap with Facetwork (fwh_osm_lz). This public copy keeps the road tiles up to zoom 7 and enlarges them beyond that, so the maps stay small enough to host here; at city zoom the lines are slightly simplified. Large countries are split by state or province. Road data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
